1. L&T MUTUAL FUND ➔ HSBC MUTUAL FUND (Acquisition & Scheme Mergers - 2022)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Acquisition Date: November 2022
• Deal Value: ~$425 Million (₹3,192 Crore)
• Background: HSBC Asset Management acquired 100% of L&T Investment Management.
• Scheme-Level Mergers & Renamings:
  - L&T Midcap Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Midcap Fund
  - L&T Flexicap Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Flexicap Fund
  - L&T Emerging Businesses Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Small Cap Fund
  - L&T Hybrid Equity Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Aggressive Hybrid Fund
  - L&T Balanced Advantage Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Balanced Advantage Fund
  - L&T Large and Midcap Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Large & Mid Cap Fund
  - L&T Tax Advantage Fund ➔ Renamed to HSBC ELSS Tax Saver Fund
  - L&T India Value Fund ➔ Renamed to HSBC Value Fund
  - L&T Infrastructure Fund ➔ Renamed to HSBC Infrastructure Fund
  - L&T India Large Cap Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Large Cap Fund
  - L&T Short Term Value Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Short Duration Fund
  - L&T Resurgent India Corporate Bond Fund ➔ Merged into HSBC Corporate Bond Fund
