EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
This research identifies Indian Portfolio Management Services (PMS) providers that
publish strategy-level, downloadable monthly factsheet PDFs on public, unauthenticated
web pages (similar to Carnelian Capital Advisors).

Most major Indian PMS providers gate their strategy factsheets behind private client/
distributor portals (e.g. Marcellus, ASK, Unifi) or submit data exclusively to the
APMI central regulatory portal. However, several top SEBI-registered PMS managers
maintain open, browsable strategy pages with direct, downloadable factsheets.

================================================================================
AUDIT OF OTHER NOTABLE PMS PROVIDERS & DISTRIBUTION PATTERNS
================================================================================

1. Marcellus Investment Managers (marcellus.in):
   - Status: Gated behind client login.
   - Detail: Marcellus lists its strategies (Consistent Compounders, Kings of Capital,
     Little Champs, Rising Giants) publicly with high-level descriptions and newsletters,
     but full monthly factsheet PDFs and portfolio holdings require distributor or
     investor portal authentication (portal.marcellus.in).

2. ASK Investment Managers (askfinancials.com):
   - Status: Gated behind investor portal.
   - Detail: Detailed performance metrics for IEP, Growth, Financial Opportunities,
     and Golden Decade are restricted behind client/distributor logins.

3. Unifi Capital (unificap.com):
   - Status: Gated / Inquiry based.
   - Detail: Strategy overviews (Blended, Spin-Off, Insider Shadow, Green Fund) are
     publicly viewable, but monthly factsheets are distributed directly to investors
     via email / relationship managers.

4. WhiteOak Capital Management (whiteoakamc.com / whiteoakindia.com):
   - Status: Aggregated monthly regulatory report rather than standalone strategy PDFs.
   - Detail: Strategy-level data is provided primarily in aggregate monthly disclosures
     and investor presentations rather than isolated standalone monthly factsheet PDFs.

5. Institutional Mutual Fund PMS Arms (Kotak PMS, ICICI Pru PMS, DSP PMS, Nippon India PMS):
   - Status: Download centers / Centralized APMI reporting.
   - Detail: Most institutional AMCs maintain dedicated PMS sections, but their factsheets
     are published under complex dynamic SPA interfaces or consolidated under annual/half-yearly
     statutory disclosures.

6. APMI Portal Centralization (apmiindia.org):
   - SEBI mandates that all registered PMS managers upload monthly performance data and
     portfolio metrics to the Association of Portfolio Managers in India (APMI).
   - If building a comprehensive, multi-provider PMS tracker across 300+ SEBI registered
     managers, aggregating from APMI directly complements scraping individual boutique sites.
